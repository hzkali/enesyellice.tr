My Journey & Vision

I'm Muhammed Enes Yellice — a software developer turned tech entrepreneur with 9+ years of hands-on experience. I started with code, and evolved into a founder who builds value-driven, scalable digital products across Web, Mobile, Web3, and HealthTech industries.
